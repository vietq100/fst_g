# ECONOMY SYSTEM

## Currency

### Gold
The only numeric currency.

Sources:
- enemy drops
- expedition completion bonus
- boss reward
- selling items (including Warehouse overflow)

Sinks:
- recruitment
- Tavern candidate refresh
- building upgrades
- crafting
- Hospital speed-up

## Materials (inventory items, not currencies)

### Iron Ore
Source: Goblins, boss.
Sinks: Iron equipment, building upgrades.

### Beast Fang
Source: Wolves, boss.
Sink: Rare equipment (Fang Blade).

### Dark Essence
Rare material.
Source: Forest Guardian (guaranteed on first clear, chance on repeat).
Sinks: Rare crafting at Blacksmith level 2, Town Hall level 3.

Do not add more currencies or materials until required.

All values: `BALANCE.md`.

## Economy principles

Every currency/material must have:
- source
- sink
- reason to exist

Avoid inflation during MVP.

## Open-ended economy

There is no endgame, so the economy must keep working indefinitely:
- Maintain gold and material sinks that scale with progress (higher-tier upgrades, larger roster, higher-rarity crafting).
- Do not rely on a final purchase that "completes" the game.
- Rare materials need sinks that remain useful at any stage.

## Care costs

Nurturing Hunters has costs that create decisions:
- Hospital speed-up
- Tavern refresh
- (later) training facilities

Costs must be balanced so that caring for Hunters is a choice, not a tax.
