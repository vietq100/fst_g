# VFX DESIGN

## Philosophy

Effects must improve feedback without obscuring gameplay.

## Combat

Basic:
- hit flash
- small impact
- damage number

Critical:
- stronger impact
- distinct sound
- larger number

Death:
- short dissolve/fall effect

## Loot

When loot drops:
- small sparkle
- item popup

## Level up

- light burst
- sound
- XP completion
- level-up notification

## Status effects (D48)

- Icon row above the head: effect colour + glyph, stack count, remaining time (or shield left) bar; blinks when about to expire. Buffs have a light top edge.
- Procedural aura per effect: bubbles (poison), flames (burn), drops (bleed), sparkles (regen, focus), orbiting stars (stun), ice ring + crystals (freeze), roots, rising/falling chevrons (buffs/debuffs), shield bubble, golden glow (invulnerable), spikes (thorns), drain wisps (lifesteal), red cross (disarm).
- Pulsing body tint for poison, burn, freeze, invulnerable.
- Floating text: effect name on apply (once per 1.5 s per effect), green "+N" heals, blue "(N)" absorbed damage, coloured numbers for damage over time and thorns, "Immune" when invulnerable.

## Boss

Boss attacks need stronger effects than normal attacks.

Do not use excessive screen shake in MVP.
