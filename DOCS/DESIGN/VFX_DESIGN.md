# VFX DESIGN

## Philosophy

Effects must improve feedback without obscuring gameplay.

## Combat

Basic:
- hit flash
- small impact
- damage number

Critical:
- stronger impact
- distinct sound
- larger number

Death:
- short dissolve/fall effect

## Loot

When loot drops:
- small sparkle
- item popup

## Level up

- light burst
- sound
- XP completion
- level-up notification

## Boss

Boss attacks need stronger effects than normal attacks.

Do not use excessive screen shake in MVP.
