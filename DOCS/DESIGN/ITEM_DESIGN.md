# ITEM DESIGN

## Visual principles

Items must be recognizable from icons.

MVP item categories:
- weapon
- armor
- material

Consumable exists as a data category but has no items in MVP.

MVP item list and stats: `BALANCE.md` → Equipment.

## Rarity (tiers, D60)

C Common · U Uncommon · R Rare · SR Super Rare · SSR Special Super Rare · UR Ultra Rare · LR Legendary Rare · MR Mythic.
The same tiers apply to Hunters, monsters and bosses. LR and Mythic are fixed in data, never rolled.

Use a consistent visual language for rarity: one colour per tier (`Tier.COLORS`) on item rims, names, Hunter badges and monster labels.

## Slots (D59)

Weapon · Helmet · Top · Pants · Boots · Necklace · Ring · Charm · Skin · Pet. Helmet, top, pants, boots and skin change the Hunter's sprite; the pet follows the Hunter.

## Weapons

MVP (Warrior only):
- Rusty Sword
- Iron Sword
- Fang Blade

Later classes:
- Hunter Bow
- Apprentice Staff

## Armor

MVP:
- Leather Armor
- Iron Armor

## Materials

MVP:
- Iron Ore
- Beast Fang
- Dark Essence (rare)

Materials are stackable inventory items, not a separate currency. Gold is the only currency.

## Icon design

Target:
- 32x32 or 48x48 UI icon
- centered object
- simple silhouette
- minimal background noise

## Equipment

Equipment should communicate:
- type
- rarity
- power
