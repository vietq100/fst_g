# ENEMY DESIGN

## 1. Enemy visual philosophy

Enemies should be readable immediately.

Each enemy needs a strong silhouette and one memorable visual characteristic.

## 2. Slime

Role:
- basic tutorial enemy

Visual:
- round blob
- simple face
- translucent or soft body
- small size

Gameplay:
- slow
- weak
- common

## 3. Goblin

Role:
- standard humanoid enemy

Visual:
- small body
- large ears
- crude weapon

Gameplay:
- balanced
- common

## 4. Wolf

Role:
- fast predator

Visual:
- four-legged
- long silhouette
- aggressive stance

Gameplay:
- fast
- medium damage
- lower defense

## 5. Boss: Forest Guardian

Role:
- first major boss

Visual:
- significantly larger
- tree/stone/monster hybrid
- glowing core or eyes

Gameplay:
- high HP
- strong attacks
- simple special ability

## 6. Enemy readability

At gameplay zoom:
- Hunter silhouette must be distinguishable from enemies.
- Boss must be immediately recognizable.
