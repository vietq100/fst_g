# BUILDING DESIGN

All five buildings are in the MVP. Costs and effects: `BALANCE.md` → Buildings.

## Town Hall

Purpose:
- central management
- town level
- Hunter capacity and Hunter level cap
- unlock progression (level 2 unlocked by first Forest Guardian clear)

Visual:
- largest building
- central position
- banner/sign
- visible entrance

## Tavern

Purpose:
- recruit Hunters (3 candidates, refresh after each expedition return or for gold)
- Hunters rest (rooms) and socialize (common room) here

Visual:
- warm lighting
- wooden structure
- large sign
- lively atmosphere

## Blacksmith

Purpose:
- craft equipment from recipes
- higher level unlocks higher rarity recipes

Visual:
- forge
- chimney
- metal/wood
- orange forge light

## Hospital

Purpose:
- timed recovery of injured Hunters
- gold can shorten recovery

Visual:
- clean/simple
- medical or healing symbol
- calm colors

## Warehouse

Purpose:
- inventory capacity (slots)
- overflow on expedition return is auto-sold at sell price

Visual:
- crates
- shelves
- reinforced doors

## Training Yard (area, not a building)

Purpose:
- location of the Training activity

Visual:
- training dummies, weapon racks, fence

## Building upgrade

Upgrades should have visible changes where possible:
- size
- roof
- decoration
- banners
- lights

Do not require a completely new art asset for every level during MVP.
