# AUDIO DESIGN

## Music

Town:
- calm fantasy
- warm
- relaxed

Wilderness:
- mysterious
- slightly tense

Boss:
- stronger rhythm
- danger

## SFX

Required MVP:
- button click
- sword hit
- enemy hit
- enemy death
- loot pickup
- level up
- building upgrade
- expedition start
- expedition complete

## Audio rules

Do not make every action loud.

Important events should have stronger feedback.
