# DUNGEON DESIGN

## Dungeon philosophy

Dungeon spaces should make progression feel dangerous and rewarding.

## MVP dungeon

Dark Forest expedition.

Sections:
1. Entrance
2. Normal enemies
3. Deeper area
4. Stronger enemy group (larger mixed Goblin/Wolf group; dedicated elite enemies are post-MVP)
5. Boss arena
6. Reward/exit

## Readability

The player should understand:
- where they started
- where danger is
- where the boss is
- where to leave

## Boss arena

Use visual framing:
- clearing
- ruins
- trees
- stone circle

The arena should be large enough for movement and combat.

## Difficulty

Difficulty should come from:
- enemy stats
- composition
- boss mechanics

Not from confusing level design.
